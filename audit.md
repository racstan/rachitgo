# Portfolio Audit

## Snapshot
- Scope: visual polish, motion, content clarity, and interaction consistency.
- Goal: keep the portfolio lean, readable, and credible without inflated claims.

## Strengths
- Clear visual identity and cohesive typography.
- Interactive elements (cursor, tilt, lab) differentiate the experience.
- Modular components make iteration fast.

## Gaps
- Some headings and stats read as placeholders rather than verified facts.
- Timeline and section flow need scroll-driven focus to avoid all content showing at once.
- Footer previously felt heavy and repetitive.
- Tech stack strip had extra framing that reduced the "floating" effect.

## Recommendations
- Use hover-driven heading variants for all major sections.
- Reveal timeline entries on scroll to reinforce progression.
- Keep the footer minimal and functional.
- Keep project categories high-level unless real case studies are available.
- Avoid unverified metrics (stars, counts, followers) unless sourced.

## Needed Inputs (for final polish)
- Real contact handles and links (email, GitHub, LinkedIn, Discord, etc.).
- Verified project list with categories (academic, work, hobby).
- Any concrete metrics you want to display (stars, followers, shipped products).
