The beginning of my path in technology: During 1st standard, after demonstrating strong interest in computers at school, I gained access to a home desktop PC, initiating hands-on exploration of operating systems and software tools.

Around age 9-10, I independently configured USB tethering to connect my desktop PC to mobile internet, establishing an early foundation in networking and connectivity.

In 3rd class, Microsoft Office was introduced; I spent the next few years mastering productivity tools, document structures, and software navigation shortcuts.

In 6th standard, HTML was introduced. I began building static HTML web pages, solidifying my interest in web software engineering.

In 8th standard, I expanded into CSS for responsive visual layouts and XML for structured data hierarchy.

In 10th standard, JavaScript and C programming were introduced. I built my first full HTML/CSS/JS applications while studying procedural logic and memory concepts in C.

After 10th standard, I pursued self-directed deep dives into Python, C, C++, and Java, developing core knowledge of object-oriented design and execution models.

Entered university pursuing B.Tech in CSE (Specialization in AI & Robotics) at Vellore Institute of Technology, Chennai.

In 1st year, focused on Data Structures & Algorithms, Python, Java, and early backend (PHP) and frontend JavaScript framework ecosystems.

In 2nd year, expanded into robotics, Laravel, ReactJS, SQL, and relational database architecture.

In 3rd year, engineered a custom programming language compiler, participated in technical hackathons, and secured a Data Science internship at JPMorgan Chase & Co.

In 4th year, authored a machine learning research paper accepted at ICSCDS 2025 for ECG AFib detection, while shipping production-grade React and Laravel applications.
