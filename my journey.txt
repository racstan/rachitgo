Ever since I was born and got consciousness. We were from a lower middle class family. So everything my parents did was extremely valuable for me. My grandparent's love, playing video game casette's with my grandfather, watching cartoons on the old TV we had. Life was a heaven for me and this was the beginning of my fascination for tech.

The beginning of my love for computers: During my 1st standard seeing my interest in Computers which were then present in my school. One day After I returned to home from school, there was a computer sitting, I wondered whose it was. But when my father came from work. I found out it was mine.

So Spent few years playing the games which my father's friend who was a hardware engineer brought for us. By the way those games were 1-2mb from miniclip. But they were something which me and my lil brother used to play. Roadrash to Zuma Delux to Paint to Writing stories in MS word. I was the only guy in the class who loved tech to this extent. 

Then when I was around 9 or 10 when google became famous and youtube too. But till this time we had only mobile internet and that was extremely limited and costly. I had seen internet only in my school computers and wondered why can't I have this on my pc. So with my little brain I found about USB tethering and then seeing Google on my pc was one of my proudest moments in my life.
